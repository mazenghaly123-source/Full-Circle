    Home/What we make

    What we make.

    Cut-and-sew clothing in knit and woven fabrics, developed and produced in Cairo. Each category below lists the minimum per colour and how long a sample takes.

    What sets us apart

    Three things brands come to us for, that most local factories cannot offer.

    Denim is our specialityJeans and denim jackets from pattern to wash, from 150 pieces per colour.
    Fabrics not on the local marketWe import denims and fabrics you will not find in Cairo, so your product does not look like everyone else's.
    Imported trims + accessoriesNew buttons, rivets, zips and labels brought in from abroad, not the same trims every brand in town uses.

    Techniques + finishing

    Available across categories. Ask on the first call for anything not listed.

    Home/About

    Fashion culture leads. Process proves it.

    Full Circle is a development and manufacturing partner for clothing brands, based in Cairo. We hold the whole chain, from the first sketch to delivered bulk, so nothing is lost between suppliers.

    Why one partner

    Most brands juggle four or five suppliers. Every hand-off is a place where quality slips and nobody owns the result.

    The usual way
    Designer/ gap /Pattern maker/ gap /Fabric supplier/ gap /Factory

    Four briefs, four timelines, and a bulk run that does not match the sample.

    With Full Circle

    One team, one brief, one timeline. The sample you approve is measured against every bulk piece.
